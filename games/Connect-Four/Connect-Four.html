Game Rules

1. Objective: Be the first player to connect 4 of your colored coins in a line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).

2. Turns: Players take turns dropping one coin into any of the 7 unfull columns.

3. Gravity: Coins fall downward, occupying the lowest available space within the chosen column.

4. Draw: If the 7x6 board fills completely without any player getting 4 in a row, the game ends in a draw.
