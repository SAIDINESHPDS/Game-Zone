How to Play Vamana-Guntalu

14 Carved Pits (Guntalu)

The board features 14 carved pits (7 on your side, 7 on your opponent's side), initially holding 5 or 6 coins each.

Counter-Clockwise Sowing

Pick up all coins from any pit on your side and distribute them one by one into subsequent pits moving counter-clockwise.

Relay Continuation

When you drop your last coin, check the next pit: if it holds coins, gather them all and keep sowing uninterrupted!

Capturing (Thattu)

If your hand empties and the next pit is empty, you capture all coins from the pit right after it into your score vault.
