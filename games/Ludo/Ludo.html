Playing on Mobile Phone

Yes, this game is 100% playable on any smartphone!

  1. Same Wi-Fi network: Find your computer's IP address and open http://<your-ip>:5500/games/Ludo/Ludo.html on your phone.
  2. Online hosting: Host this folder for free on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel and open the link on mobile.
  3. Full Screen App Mode: In Safari/Chrome on phone, tap Share or Menu → tap "Add to Home Screen" to play without browser address bars!
