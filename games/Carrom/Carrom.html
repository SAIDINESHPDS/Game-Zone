Official Carrom Rules

1. Board Clearance Mode: To win, a player must sink all 9 of their coins AND the Pink Queen must be pocketed & covered.

2. Pink Queen (★) Rules:
• You can sink the Pink Queen, but you must cover it by pocketing one of your own coins on the immediate next shot (or in the same strike).
• If you fail to cover it, or commit a foul, the Queen returns to the center circle.

3. Direct Baseline Placement: Click or drag your cursor anywhere across your baseline to place your striker. Pull back to choose angle and power.

4. Fouls: Sinking the striker returns 1 of your pocketed carrom men to the center and forfeits your turn.
