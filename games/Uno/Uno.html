UNO Match Setup

Official Classic UNO Rules

1. Starting Hand: Each player starts with 7 cards.

2. Matching Cards: Match the center card by color, number, or action symbol.

3. Draw Two (+2): Next player draws 2 cards and skips their turn.

4. Reverse: Reverses rotation. In a 2-player game, acts as a Skip.

5. Skip: Next player loses their turn.

6. Wild: Can be played anytime; choose the active color.

7. Wild Draw Four (+4) & Challenge: Officially, +4 is only legal if you have NO card matching the current color. If challenged:
• Illegal Play: The +4 player draws 4 penalty cards.
• Legal Play: The challenger draws 6 cards (4 + 2 penalty).

8. UNO Shout: When playing your second-to-last card, click UNO!. If caught before the next turn, draw 2 penalty cards.
