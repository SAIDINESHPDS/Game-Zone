📜 Ashta-Chamma Rules

🐚 Cowrie Points & Names:

  • 1 mouth up: 1(Kannu)
  • 2 mouths up: 2(Duga)
  • 3 mouths up: 3(Theeni)
  • 4 mouths up: 4(Chamma) ★ — Extra Throw
  • 0 mouths up: 8(Astha) ★ — Extra Throw

🔓 Individual Player Unlock Rule: Every player must personally roll 4(Chamma) or 8(Astha) to qualify. Until unlocked, you cannot move any coins (even coins entered by a teammate)!

⛔ Stopping at the Cross (Gaddi): Coins without a registered kill CANNOT advance past the crossed safe square (Step 12) located 3 boxes before the home corner. They must halt at the cross and wait until an opponent coin is captured before entering the inner sanctum.

⚡ Automatic Movement: If a player has only one movable coin on the board and rolls 1(Kannu), 2(Duga), or 3(Theeni), it advances automatically!

🟢 ASHTA (8) Entry Rules:

  • No coins on board: Exactly 2 coins enter the board (they don't move 8 spaces). The player rolls again to decide which coin to move!
  • Exactly 1 coin outside: That coin enters the board and moves 4 steps (mandatory) + Extra Throw.
  • No coins outside: Select any eligible coin on the board to move 8 steps + Extra Throw.

🔴 CHAMMA (4) Entry Rules:

  • 1 or more outside: Exactly 1 token enters the board (does not move 4 spaces) + Extra Throw.
  • 0 outside: Player selects which eligible coin advances 4 spaces + Extra Throw.

🤝 4-Player 2 Teams: 8 coins vs 8 coins. Both players of a team share the exact same color. Opposite boxes (Top & Bottom or Left & Right) are used.

⚔️ Mandatory Kill Rule: A capture is required to enter the inner ring towards the goal.

✖ Safe Cells (Gaddi): Tokens on any of the 9 crossed squares cannot be captured.
