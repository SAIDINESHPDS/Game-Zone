Official Puli Meka Rules

1. Setup: 3 Tigers start on the board (1 at apex, 2 at the adjacent inner nodes). All 15 Goats start in hand.

2. First Move: Goats play first.

3. Phase 1 (Goat Placement): The Goat player places one goat per turn on any empty node. Placed goats cannot move until all 15 have entered.

4. Tiger Movement: During both phases, tigers can step 1 space along drawn lines, or jump over an adjacent goat into an empty space directly behind it to capture it.

5. Phase 2 (Movement): Once all 15 goats are on the board, goats slide 1 step per turn along connected lines. Goats cannot jump or capture.

6. Single Captures & Turns: Tigers capture exactly 1 goat per move and do not get a bonus turn.

7. Winning: Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win by surrounding and immobilizing all 3 tigers.

8. Draw: If the exact same board position repeats 3 times, the game ends in a draw.
