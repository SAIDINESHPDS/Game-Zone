How to Play Pachisi

6 Cowrie Shells Scoring

1 Mouth Up: 10 points + Extra Turn
2, 3, 4 Mouths Up: 2, 3, 4 points
5 Mouths Up: 25 points (Pachisi) + Extra Turn
6 Mouths Up (All Mouths): 12 points + Extra Turn
0 Mouths Up (All Backs): 6 points + Extra Turn

Starter Pawns Active

Every player starts with 1 pawn already on their starting square to begin racing immediately!

Entering New Pawns

Roll a bonus throw (6, 10, 12, or 25 points) to enter a new pawn from your Charkoni onto your starting square.

Castles & Sanctuaries ("X" Squares)

Squares marked with an X are safe sanctuaries where pawns cannot be cut.

Cutting / Capturing

Landing on an opponent's pawn cuts it back to their Charkoni and grants an extra throw! You must cut at least one pawn to enter the home path.

Reaching Home (Charkoni)

The first player to navigate all 4 pawns back to the central Charkoni wins the match!
