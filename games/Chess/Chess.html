Official Chess Rules

1. Objective: Checkmate the opponent King so it cannot escape attack.

2. Movement:
• Pawn (♟): 1 square forward (or 2 on initial move). Captures 1 square diagonally forward.
• Knight (♞): L-shaped movement (2+1 squares). Jumps over pieces.
• Bishop (♝): Diagonal paths across same-colored squares.
• Rook (♜): Straight horizontal and vertical paths.
• Queen (♛): Combines movements of Rook and Bishop.
• King (♚): 1 square in any direction. Must never enter check.

3. Castling: If neither King nor Rook has moved, and passing squares are safe, King slides 2 squares towards Rook and Rook jumps over.

4. En Passant: A pawn advancing 2 squares past an opposing pawn can be captured diagonally on the immediate next turn.

5. Draw Conditions: Stalemate, 3-fold repetition, 50-move rule, or insufficient material (K vs K, K+B vs K, K+N vs K).
